ATS RULES FOR TECH RESUMES
- Use standard section headings: Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education, Certifications.
- Mirror exact keywords from the job description (e.g. "Amazon EC2", "CI/CD", "Kubernetes").
- Put quantified results in bullets (e.g. "deploys in under 5 minutes").
- Use a single-column layout. Avoid tables, text boxes and images for key content.
- List certifications with the full name and code.
- Keep to 1 page for freshers.
